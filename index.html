01 /About

Grounded in geography. Curious about intelligence.

I am Zhilin Xue (薛知林), an undergraduate in Geographical Science at the School of Geographical Sciences, Changchun Normal University.

My current work examines how urban expansion relates to the spatial patterns of land surface temperature, and how multiple environmental risks can be characterized through remote sensing and GIS. I use interpretable machine learning to better understand the associations between landscape factors and urban thermal conditions.

I am also exploring a transition toward more fundamental computational research—particularly spatial intelligence, 3D scene understanding, multimodal learning, and intelligent agents. These are emerging interests, not claims of completed research.

ONGOING RESEARCH

Urban Expansion & Thermal Spatial Patterns

A Changchun case study comparing multi-period summer land surface temperature composites with changes in the urban landscape. Random Forest and SHAP are used to explore associations between spatial factors and thermal patterns.
