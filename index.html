Selected research & projects

Urban Expansion & Thermal Spatial Patterns

A Changchun case study comparing multi-period summer land surface temperature composites with changes in the urban landscape. Random Forest and SHAP are used to explore associations between spatial factors and thermal patterns.

Compound Urban Heat–Flood Risk & Exposure

Integrating thermal risk, runoff-related spatial indicators, and building-use proxies to identify urban areas where multiple environmental pressures overlap.

Urban Spatial Planning Scenario Simulation

A geography-learning prototype where learners explore the potential effects of land-use and infrastructure choices. My contribution centers on GIS data processing, spatial indicators, and map-ready data outputs.
